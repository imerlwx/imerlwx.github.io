Hi there! 😃

I am a PhD student at the City University of Hong Kong working on Human-Computer Interaction. My research explores the collaborative loop between humans and AI, asking how we can build systems that can assist us express our goals, correctly infer our goals and provide guidance. I especially care about **how people express their intent beyond the limits of plain text**. By combining technical system building with empirical evaluation, I design interfaces that let AI accurately infer our intents and translate them into structured, interactive formats, focusing particularly on the rich modality of speech. My recent work spans interactive node-link canvases for clarifying spoken thoughts, speech-driven tools that support the creative writing process, and learner-centered pedagogical agents that adaptively guide students.
