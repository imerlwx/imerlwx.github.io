Wengxi Li (李翁西)

Second-year PhD Student @ CityUHK (HK PhD Fellowship Recipient)

Hi there! 😃

People’s goals are often richer than what they can put into words. My research focuses on the gap between what people intend and what AI systems can actually perceive. I build and study interactive systems that help people externalize their goals more fully, and help AI infer those goals more accurately – with a particular focus on speech as an expressive medium that remains underexplored in HCI.

My work approaches this from both sides. On the human side, I design interfaces that scaffold intent expression: giving people richer ways to articulate what they want beyond typing a prompt. On the AI side, I study how systems can be built to interpret that richer input and translate it into structured, actionable outputs. Recent projects include an interactive node-link canvas for clarifying spoken thoughts, speech-driven tools for creative writing, and adaptive pedagogical agents that infer and respond to learner needs.

I am actively seeking for internships starting in Fall 2026. If you’d like to get in touch, please don’t hesitate to contact me via email, or on X/LinkedIn!

Previously, I am a research assistant at Stanford University, supervised by Hari Subramonyam at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI). I got my Master’s degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan. I also got a Master’s degree in Medical Physics at the University College London. My undergrad was at the Beijing Normal University, where I studied Physics.

I’ve also worked on a few fun coding projects, which you can view via my GitHub profile.
